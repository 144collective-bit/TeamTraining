## Quality management and competence evidence

- [ISO 9001 Clause 7.2 — Competence (ISMS.online)](https://www.isms.online/iso-9001/clause-7-2-competence/)
- [Defining ISO 9001:2015 Clause 7.2 Competence (Auditor Training Online)](https://blog.auditortrainingonline.com/blog/defining-iso-9001-clause-7.2-competence)
- [ISO 9001 clause 7.2 competency — three questions to ask (Ideagen)](https://www.ideagen.com/thought-leadership/blog/iso-9001-clause-72-competency-three-questions-to-ask)
- [ISO 9001 Clause 7.2/7.3: Competence, Training & Awareness (Ecesis)](https://www.ecesis.net/ISO-9001/Competence-and-Awareness.aspx)

Key points used: records of training, certificates, licences and on-the-job training are
the expected evidence; no prescribed format, but records must be retrievable on request;
a common audit finding is records showing attendance but not assessment.

## UK health & safety record-keeping

- [H&S Record Keeping Requirements Guide (Safety Clarity)](https://safetyclarity.co.uk/articles/workplace-safety/health-safety-record-keeping)
- [How long should health & safety records be kept? (Shredall SDS Group)](https://www.shredall.co.uk/blogs/blog/health-safety-records-management)
- [UK Health and Safety Retention Guide (Featherstone Safety Hub)](https://www.featherstonesafetyhub.co.uk/guides/health-and-safety-record-retention)
- [COSHH Records: The 5-Employee Threshold (Logbook)](https://www.logbook.co.uk/coshh-assessment-records-what-uk-employers-must-keep/)

Key points used: Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 requires
recording significant findings of risk assessments where there are 5+ employees; no fixed
statutory retention for training records but they must be defensible; hazardous-substance
exposure records can require retention up to 40 years; absence of records invites the
inference that training did not occur.

## Electronic signatures — UK law

- [Electronic signature laws and validity in the UK (Juro)](https://juro.com/esignature-legality/uk)
- [Electronic Signatures in England and Wales (LexisNexis UK)](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-gb/legal/guidance/electronic-signatures)

- [Training Within Industry (Lean Enterprise Institute)](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/training-within-industry-twi/)
- [Job Instruction (TWI-JI) — TPS Encyclopedia (Art of Lean)](https://artoflean.com/reference/job-instruction/)

## Industrial engineering — standard time and OEE

- [Standard time in manufacturing (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_time_in_manufacturing)
- [PFD allowance (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PFD_allowance)
- [Standard Time, Rating Factor and Allowances (Engineering with Management)](https://engineeringwithmanagement.com/standard-time-rating-factor-and-types-of-allowances/)
- [OEE Calculation: Definitions, Formulas, Examples (OEE.com)](https://www.oee.com/calculating-oee/)
- [Understanding OEE in Lean Manufacturing (Lean Production)](https://www.leanproduction.com/oee/)
- [The complete guide to OEE: formula, benchmarks (Guidewheel)](https://www.guidewheel.com/blog/oee-calculation)

Key points used: `ST = OT × R × (1 + PF&D)`; typical industrial allowances 10–20%; OEE =
Availability × Performance × Quality; 85% world-class benchmark for discrete
manufacturing against a typical 55–65%.

## Learning curves

- [Experience curve effects (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experience_curve_effects)
- [Workforce Learning Curves for Human-Based Assembly Operations (Applied Sciences, MDPI)](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/19/9608)
- [Task-based competence development: learning and forgetting in production ramp-up (Production Engineering)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11740-026-01440-8)
- [The Impact of Work Instruction Simplification on Operator Performance and Learning Curve Efficiency (Springer)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-03515-8_11)

Key points used: Wright's law and the DeJong extension with an incompressibility factor;
learning factors typically between −0.15 and −0.5, varying with how manual the task is;
training plans built around learning curves accelerate production ramp-up.
